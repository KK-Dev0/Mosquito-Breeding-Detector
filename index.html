    Backyard Sweep finds containers that can hold water. It can't tell if there is water inside, so check each one by eye.

    How a sweep works

    1. Walk the edge of your home and yard, or your street.
    2. Photograph anything that could hold water: tires, vases, bottles, coconut shells and drains.
    3. Scan each photo. Tip out, scrub or bin whatever gets flagged.

    What it can and can't do

    It recognises five kinds of container: tires, vases, bottles, coconut shells and drain inlets. It can't tell whether they hold water.

    The model is YOLOv8n, trained on 1,828 photos from the open VisText-Mosquito dataset. On that dataset's held-out test photos it scored mAP50 0.926 (183 images). Those photos come from the same collection as the training set, so expect lower accuracy on your own street.
